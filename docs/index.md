# AIchemy Hub Research Data

This site contains an up-to-date collection of research data produced by or affiliated to the AIchemy Hub.
It is intended to be a resource for researchers, students, and practitioners in the field of AI for chemistry.

Links to raw datasets, data repositories, and data-related resources are included where available.

Browse the **[Research Data](./research-data.md)** page for an overview of the datasets currently available from the AIchemy Hub.

Take a look at our **[Licensing Guidelines](./licensing-guidelines.md)** for advice on how to share your research data with a clear licence.
